Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how Autoclipper ("the App") handles data. The App is a private tool used only by its owner to upload clips to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data of users. The App has no user accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Access to TikTok and YouTube

  • The App asks only for the permissions needed to upload videos to the owner's accounts: TikTok user.info.basic and video.upload (videos are sent to the TikTok drafts inbox), and YouTube youtube.upload.
  • The App does not read, store or analyze videos, comments, followers or any other data of other TikTok or YouTube users.

OAuth tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer or server only. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok and Google when the App uploads a video or refreshes a token.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Video files are sent only to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time: in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) and in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access). On request we delete stored tokens and upload logs within 7 days.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
